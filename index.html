Rise of the Red Moon is a battle royale game developed by team DiLupo in November 2025 as a joint final project submission for the classes Game Networking and Artificial Intelligence in Games. The players are pitted against each other, growing their pack of wolves before the Red Moon rises, which is when they transform and fight until only 1 is left.

Language: C#
Engine: Unity
Platform: Windows

Contribution: Wolf AI, multiplayer capabilities (Photon)
